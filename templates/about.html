Built for Indian
University Students

ReportGen was born from a simple frustration — hours wasted writing internship reports from scratch when students should be focused on learning.

"Every student deserves a professional report, regardless of their writing skills."

We built ReportGen to level the playing field. Whether you're at GTU or SPPU, whether you're in computer science or commerce, our AI writes a complete, formatted, university-ready internship report based on your inputs — in minutes, not days.

The Problem We Solve

Every year, thousands of Indian university students spend 10–30 hours writing mandatory internship reports. Many struggle with formal writing, proper formatting, and meeting university guidelines. Some pay others to write for them. Most submit below-standard reports and face consequences.

ReportGen changes this completely. You fill in what you actually did — your company, your tasks, your learnings — and our AI writes a professional, well-formatted report tailored to your university's structure.
