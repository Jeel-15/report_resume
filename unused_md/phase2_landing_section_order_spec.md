# Phase 2 Landing Section Order Spec

Date: 2026-05-13

## Current Order

1. Hero
2. Speed strip
3. How it works
4. Preview
5. Conversion band CTA
6. Stats
7. Testimonials
8. Pricing
9. Final CTA

## Conversion Intent

- The hero carries the primary promise and the first action.
- The speed strip provides immediate proof and product scope.
- The how-it-works section reduces uncertainty with a simple process.
- The preview section shows the output before the user commits.
- The conversion band repeats the primary CTA at a strategic mid-page point.
- Stats, testimonials, and pricing add validation before the final CTA.

## Notes

- The visual theme remains unchanged: dark premium landing with blue accents and soft gradients.
- The new conversion band is intentionally lightweight so it reinforces action without adding a new large section type.
- Motion is now softened for users who prefer reduced motion.
