Refund Policy

Our refund and cancellation terms for paid plans.

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Free Plan

The free plan is provided at no cost and does not involve any payment. No refunds are applicable.

2. Paid Plans — Eligibility for Refund

We offer refunds under the following conditions:

  • 7-day money-back guarantee: If you are not satisfied with a paid plan, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your original purchase, provided you have not used more than 1 report generation credit.
  • Technical failure: If a report generation fails due to a technical error on our end and we are unable to resolve it within 48 hours, you are entitled to a full refund of the affected credit.
  • Duplicate charges: If you were charged twice for the same transaction, we will refund the duplicate charge immediately upon verification.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • You have successfully generated and downloaded a report or resume.
  • More than 7 days have passed since the purchase date.
  • Refund request is for subscription renewals that were not cancelled before the renewal date.
  • Account was suspended due to violation of our Terms of Service.
  • Change of mind after the 7-day window has passed.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email support@reportgen.in with:

  • Your registered email address
  • Transaction ID or payment reference number
  • Reason for the refund request

We will review and respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

5. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not provide a refund for the current billing period, unless you are within the 7-day window.

6. Contact

For refund queries, contact us at support@reportgen.in or call us at +91 99999 99999 (Mon–Sat, 10 AM – 7 PM IST).
