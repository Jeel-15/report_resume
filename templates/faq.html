About the Platform
What is ReportGen?+
ReportGen is an AI-powered platform that helps Indian university students generate professional internship reports and resumes in minutes. You fill in details about your internship, and our AI writes a complete, formatted report tailored to your university's requirements.
Which universities are supported?+
We currently support 50+ Indian universities including GTU (Gujarat Technological University), SPPU (Savitribai Phule Pune University), Mumbai University, Anna University, VTU, and many more. Each university has custom report section templates configured by our academic team. If your university is not listed, contact us and we'll add it.
How long does report generation take?+
Report generation typically takes 1–3 minutes. Our AI writes each section simultaneously for speed. You'll see the status update in real time on your dashboard.
Is it plagiarism to use AI-generated reports?+
This depends on your university's academic integrity policy. ReportGen generates content based on YOUR specific internship details — it is not copied from any existing source. Every report is unique to you. However, we strongly recommend reviewing and editing the generated content to add your personal experience and voice. Using the platform to submit work that is entirely AI-generated without review may violate your institution's policies. Use ReportGen as a writing assistant, not a replacement for your own experience.
Using the Platform
Can I edit the generated report?+
Yes, absolutely. Every section of the generated report is fully editable. You can modify any text, add your own content, upload images, and preview the final PDF before downloading. The report editor is built into the platform.
What does the downloaded PDF look like?+
The PDF includes a professional cover page with your name, roll number, college name, and company logos. It follows standard academic formatting with proper section headings, page numbers, and consistent typography. Visit our sample output section on the homepage to see an example.
What is the Resume Builder?+
The Resume Builder is a separate feature that helps you create a professional single-page resume. You fill in your education, experience, skills, projects, and certifications in a step-by-step form, and the platform generates a cleanly formatted PDF resume ready for job applications. AI Enhance buttons can improve each section with professional language.
Account & Pricing
Is there a free plan?+
Yes! The free plan allows you to generate 1 internship report. This is enough for most students who need a single report per semester. Paid plans offer more report generations and priority support. See our pricing section for details.
Can I generate multiple reports?+
Yes. With paid plans you can generate multiple reports — useful if you have multiple internship semesters or want to start fresh after editing. Each generated report is saved to your account and can be accessed anytime.
How do I reset my password?+
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your registered email and you'll receive an OTP to reset your password. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.
